# Unify `work-enumeration` and `arms-speak`, then spend on one thing at a time

*Plan written 2026-09-23 after both sessions' runs finished. develop at `2a1f1c9`; `work-enumeration` at `0cfb162`; `arms-speak` at `8d4cf6e`. To be copied verbatim to `docs/plans/2026-09-23-unify-the-contract-branches.md` as the first commit of the unification.*

## Context

Two sessions built the same idea in parallel today: make the specialist arms account for every target and let them file an ask without a compiling fix. `work-enumeration` built it tool-side (`target_verdicts`: every target gets `issue` / `no_issue` / `cannot_determine` with a reason; `verify_edits_if_present` stamped after the agenda is built; prompts untouched at `focused-prompt/4`) and paid for one matched pair on four PRs. `arms-speak` built it prompt-side too (`target_abstentions`; the contract text rewritten: an ask-without-fix clause, "Account for every target", "Two strengths of ask" with `advisory` defined and "a wrong finding costs this review far more than a silence does" removed; a `severity` description; a naming-prompt correction) and halted before spending when it found the other run in flight. Both fork from the same develop commit; they collide in nine files, and a naive merge leaves `runner.py` with two `submission_verification_policy` fields where the later, `Optional` one wins and `None` flows into `RenderedPrompt`.

**What the paid pair says** (`pr5_V2_verdicts_fanout4_rep1` vs `_control_rep1`, fanout, no generalist, PRs 33145 / 33315 control / 33337 / 33421, 10 obligations, one rep each):

| | treatment | control |
|---|---|---|
| issue recall, majority / unanimous | 3 / 10, 2 / 10 | 2 / 10, 1 / 10 |
| jobs filing | 49 of 139 | 38 of 140 |
| candidates; on control PR 33315 | 87; **0** | 57; 1 |
| billed | $5.82 | $4.74 |

The one obligation the treatment "gained" (e83e6544) is a judge-vote flip: the control's naming arm filed nearly the same rename and drew 1 of 3 votes against 2 of 3. Measured by what the arms filed, the verdict contract gained no obligation; it raised filing from 27% to 35%, added 30 candidates at zero control-PR cost, and put four arms instead of one on bd8a8d8c. Every target shown was answered (415 of 415), `cannot_determine` was chosen 0 times, and one job paused on the $0.17 cap and lost its answer while the manifest reported `paused: 0` (fanout rows carry no status, so those counters always read 0). Of the seven obligations neither run hit, three are `naming_norm` limits (suffix rename, `coe_` still leading, `subject_unresolved` answered `no_issue`) and three are the right site with the wrong ask.

**The finding that changes the plan's shape.** `work-enumeration`'s Phase 0 (`docs/research/the-question-is-already-asked-2026-09.md`, on that branch) found the current briefs already ask 17 of the 23 held-out questions, and of the 42 obligation-reps missed where a brief asks it, **25 were pruned by the lead** (the arm was never run) and 15 were silence. On the held-out twelve, pruning loses more than silence, so a lead-shaped run can only act on what survives pruning. And the control alone, specialists with no generalist and no lead on current prompts, reached 2 of 10 there, what the generalist-anchored fanout reached on 2026-09-14.

**Decisions taken (user, 2026-09-23):** one per-target mechanism survives, `target_verdicts`, with `arms-speak`'s contract text ported onto the verdict vocabulary and `target_abstentions` deleted; knobs are opt-in so the committed control stays byte-reproducible; the first paid run after unification is the 4-PR fanout with the unified treatment against the already-paid V2 control, about $6; reps 2–3 of the old verdict treatment are retired as superseded.

**Standing constraints.** Selection is parked (`docs/dead-ends.md`, "Publication as selection"): every number is pre-gate, with control-PR emission beside it. Paid runs go one at a time, with the key check and the first-minutes watch from `CLAUDE.md` "Providers". Three reps before any rate is quoted; one rep is a level, provisional.
