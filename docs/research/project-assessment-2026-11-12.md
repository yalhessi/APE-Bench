# Mathlib review research assessment

12 November 2026. Repository snapshot: `develop@9dbcec63`, with the relevant experiment worktrees inspected separately. This assessment concerns the research programme and its next investments. It does not introduce a reviewer implementation or authorize a model-backed experiment.

**The project has established a real gap between technically competent review and Mathlib review decisions, but it has not established a transferable improvement over its strongest measured solo reviewer.** The evidence supports continuing a narrower investigation of learning from prior review decisions. The immediate priority is to improve how that investigation reaches a decision: finish measuring the value of delivered comments, spend more runs on independent PRs, and require a new intervention to add information or a demonstrably useful operation to the solo baseline.

Cost constraints are real, but small experiments are only part of the problem. The programme repeatedly invests in explaining familiar misses, then stops before completing the outcome assessment or testing transfer broadly. Its infrastructure and audit practices are substantial assets. What is missing is a sufficiently short route from a hypothesis to an independent comparison that can close it.

**The strongest findings are more specific than either “the models cannot review” or “the harness only needs better conventions.”** The following measurements retain their original populations and instruments; their percentages should not be pooled.

| Evidence | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| The residual-mechanism reading found object inspection in 63 of 67 missed asks; only four were never attempted | Merely arranging for the code to be read is unlikely to solve most of these misses | Reading an object is not evidence that the right alternative, comparison or proof trial was considered |
| Exact applicable-convention supply raised Claude's in-census recovery from roughly 46–49% to 74% | External information can substantially change review behaviour | The effect of learning a rule is not isolated from telling the reviewer which rule matters on this PR |
| Ask-free selection recovered the relevant entry for 22 of 33 asks; bare delivery added about three percentage points, and later gains transferred inconsistently | Retrieval recall and useful application are separate problems | Increasing shortlist recall alone will deliver the oracle gain |
| A forced-filing design reached none of 32 previously never-reached asks and increased comment volume | Suppression is not a sufficient general explanation | Every model or every already-constructed candidate has the same loss stage |
| Four Opus passes reached 12 of 23 obligations but produced seven findings per control PR; agreement AUC was about 0.48 | Additional calls buy some coverage and substantial burden | Agreement is a validated usefulness filter, or historical nonasks are necessarily wrong |
| The latest compact/full knowledge experiment reached none of its three primary concerns in any arm | The present bank failed its registered advancement requirement | Its complete usefulness comparison is finished, or all reusable knowledge is ineffective |
| Focused generality sessions constructed and compiled alternatives in all six target answers; two unbriefed answers requested a generalization and none of the three note-bearing answers did | Construction and recommendation can fail separately | The note generally harms review, or a changed filing instruction will solve the problem |

These findings are documented in the [question register](../questions.md), the [knowledge-transfer synthesis](knowledge-transfer-experiment-synthesis-2026-10-23.md), and the [focused-application assessment](review-harness-next-question-2026-10-27.md). The last two distinguish the conditional positives from the failures of transfer.

The latest focused cases also resist a single diagnosis. Namespace focus exposed the intended alternative in two note-bearing answers, which then declined it. Generality construction succeeded, but the final recommendation differed from the historical review. The automation question targeted registration, whereas the missed historical request involved using `grind [definition]` to simplify proofs. That last result cannot establish failure at the untested proof-simplification question. These are observable actions and stated reasons, not direct measurements of the model's internal judgement.

**The main measurement gap is that historical agreement still carries more weight than demonstrated reviewing value.** Historical comments provide valuable independent positive examples. They do not enumerate every useful comment, and an unchanged piece of code does not identify an explicit decision to retain it. Nor is every historical suggestion a required revision: discussions include questions, optional improvements and suggestions later withdrawn or qualified.

Three objectives have repeatedly become entangled: reproducing a recorded request, producing a worthwhile comment, and deciding whether a PR is acceptable. They need separate outcomes. A review can be useful without reproducing the historical comment. It can reproduce a tentative comment that the discussion later rejects. It can offer a useful improvement while correctly accepting the existing contribution.

The [491-item usefulness panel](../../.claude/worktrees/reusable-review-knowledge/results/pr_review_v5/adjudications/reusable-review-knowledge-2026-10-24/README.md) still contains a response template but no completed response file, and `eval/concerns.json` is absent. The [results reading](../../.claude/worktrees/reusable-review-knowledge/docs/research/reusable-review-knowledge-results-reading-2026-10-25.md) identifies potentially useful `dslope` suggestions outside the primary targets, but leaves their value unadjudicated. The earlier [reconstruction additions panel](../../.claude/worktrees/useful-additions-triage/docs/research/phase-a-additions-triage-2026-10-23.md) also awaits the user's labels.

The correct result is therefore a failed advancement test with an unfinished broader quality assessment. Neither declaring the bank successful from 41.1% versus 35.6%, nor declaring every extra comment useless, is justified.

I would revise the earlier choice to defer all limited human calibration until the agent becomes better. Without some independent judgement at the “true but not worth requesting” boundary, the project cannot reliably tell when it has become better. This does not require a large recruited-maintainer study immediately. Start with a bounded, blinded reading by the project's qualified readers; use a small maintainer check for disputed boundaries when feasible. Keep researcher judgement, model judgement and observed maintainer decisions separately attributed. A model agreement score cannot substitute for that distinction.

**The programme spends too much of its effective sample budget on familiar PRs.** Repetition was a sensible response to real stochastic variation. However, repeating a handful of PRs estimates variability on those PRs, not transfer to different mathematical topics, authors, reviewers or contribution shapes. Multiple asks from one PR and several repetitions of one ask are also correlated observations.

The concentration is documented: one reviewer supplied 29 of the 47 development asks, and naming was substantially overrepresented relative to the 1,000-PR census. “Held-out 12” is now a historical name for development material. The project already has [separate 60-PR validation and test releases](../todo/eval-set-selection.md), including a separately reported AI-generated stratum. Those are assets to audit and use, not reasons to create another overlapping small benchmark.

For perspective, 54 whole-review sessions could cover 27 PRs once in two conditions, instead of six PRs in three conditions and three repetitions. This is a session-count comparison, not a claim of equal cost: PR size and context consumption must enter the budget. It nevertheless illustrates the allocation choice. The bank study's five extra historical ask occurrences came from two concerns on two PRs, not five independent demonstrations of transfer.

I recommend one paired pass on a broader, preselected validation batch, with repetitions on a smaller random subset to estimate stochastic variation. Use saved variance to choose the allocation. Keep both conditions for each PR together in analysis, average repetitions within PR, and obtain uncertainty by resampling PRs rather than treating every ask or session as independent. Report both average-PR outcomes and pooled ask recovery. Check whether removing one PR or one heavily represented reviewer changes the conclusion. A small batch is a screening stage; a wide interval is inconclusive, not proof of equivalence.

The test release should remain sealed until a candidate, comparison rule and evaluator are frozen. Before using it, audit exposure through prior studies, source banks, retrieval and cross-references. Development on historical answers is legitimate; reporting that development as independent validation is the error. Similarly, a source bank selected because it addresses familiar misses is a developmental intervention even when its own sources exclude the target PRs.

**Several apparent judgement failures were failures of the rule or the measurement.** The convention programme has already shown that one-sentence rules can reverse a priority, omit a condition or describe a practice more broadly than maintainers actually apply it. The corrected namespace reading put roughly 85–97% of the relevant unasked survivors outside the claimed departure class. Primed-name rules failed their held-out calibration under more than one wording. Dot-notation departures often were genuine and remained unasked. These cases require different explanations; they cannot all support a global claim that models disregard valid conventions. See the [October synthesis](knowledge-transfer-experiment-synthesis-2026-10-23.md) and [departure study](../../results/pr_review_v5/probes/enforcement-at-departures-2026-10-16/RESULTS.md).

The historical feedback is also selectively observed. A reviewer may not inspect a site; an author may avoid a known problem before submitting; a file may retain older practice; a later edit may change the line for another reason. A nearby comment is evidence of attention, but not proof that the reviewer evaluated every alternative there. “Unasked,” “retained after explicit discussion,” and “accepted as preferable” must remain different labels. Counting all unasked occurrences as negatives would train the project to reproduce attention and selection effects alongside review preferences.

This changes what it means to discover a convention. The useful object is a supported decision about alternatives in a defined setting: what was proposed, why it was preferred or declined, which consequences mattered, who decided, when it applied, and what would make the decision different. A general rule is one possible compression of such decisions. Its validity must be checked separately from whether supplying it improves review.

**The oracle result is encouraging, but it is not a deployment recipe.** Exact applicable-convention supply removes uncertainty about both relevance and intervention. It can tell the model that an otherwise acceptable-looking object merits another look. A deployable method must discover that opportunity, recover appropriate evidence, construct the right alternative, compare its consequences and decide whether to comment. Improving any one stage does not guarantee an end-to-end gain.

There is also a source of ambiguity in the proposed target. “The Mathlib judgement” is not necessarily one deterministic policy shared by every maintainer at every date. Local interface costs, scope, evolving practice and reviewer priorities can differ. The project should test how much of that variation is predictable and how much requires an unresolved or optional answer. An observed disagreement with one historical request is evidence of disagreement, not automatically a technical error or a failure of the community as a whole.

The difficulty of improving a frontier model does not establish a capability ceiling. Many tested interventions ask essentially the same model to reconsider the same information with more structure. A strong solo reviewer already performs some of those investigations, so duplication can cost more without changing its evidence. A plausible advantage must come from something concrete: prior project decisions it did not recover, a verified search it did not perform, or calibration learned from independent outcomes. Each needs a measured incremental effect. Replacing the baseline with a weaker or cheaper model would answer a different question.

**The next substantive research question should be whether prior decisions provide transferable information about review value.** This extends the October 27 decision diagnostic, but it should not become another prolonged revision of the notes for `fract`, the namespace case or `to_fun`.

First assemble a bounded panel of independent, explicit choices about feasible alternatives, including both requests for changes and reasoned retention of the existing form. Generality is a reasonable initial family because construction is already demonstrated. Library-mechanism tradeoffs are another candidate if their sources are clearer. Choose using source availability and real contrary cases, not which familiar target offers the easiest win. If sufficient independent decisions cannot be recovered, that is a reason to stop the proposed learning experiment or obtain new feedback.

At this stage, hold candidate alternatives and checked technical consequences fixed. Compare the same strong model with ordinary context against access to eligible prior decision evidence; keep inference resources comparable. The diagnostic may also compare concrete source decisions with the existing compressed note, if that distinction is necessary to locate the loss. This is not a claim that examples are a new solution: example injection, retrieval, precedence paragraphs and richer notes have already been tried. The unresolved issue is whether independently supported decision boundaries predict new decisions, including meaningful counterexamples, before another whole-review integration.

Read preferred implementation, useful comment, optional versus required change, and concrete proposed action separately. Preserve unresolved cases. Use independent PRs and groups of related decisions in the split; deduplicate repeated renames, backports and discussions citing the same decision. Date all source evidence before the target episode, including discussion resolutions. Within a fixed-alternative diagnostic, showing the alternatives is intentional conditioning, not evidence of autonomous discovery.

| Diagnostic result | Next investment |
|---|---|
| Independent evidence improves decisions on new PRs and handles feasible contrary cases | Test a bounded way to retrieve and apply that evidence during whole review |
| Raw decisions help but their summary does not | Investigate information lost during acquisition or compression |
| Decisions improve with supplied candidates, but whole review fails to generate them | Investigate candidate activation or targeted search, with candidate volume measured |
| Benefits occur only on familiar targets, or merely shift all answers toward “request” | Close this intervention; do not repair it against the same evaluation cases |
| Neither evidence representation improves independent decisions | Do not build a larger bank or retrieval platform on this premise |

A whole-review candidate needs two comparisons when it consumes substantial extra resources: the normal strong solo baseline, and ordinary solo work with a comparable additional budget. Additional investigation, preparation and selection all count. An effect over a deliberately weak model would not answer the current question. The strongest measured route is itself an experimental condition: the repository records a material Opus route effect, so switching harnesses, tool visibility or reasoning transport while changing knowledge would confound the result.

Budget the complete study, including source acquisition, unsuccessful attempts, evaluation and researcher time. Report one-time acquisition separately from per-PR inference and state how it is amortized. Subscription quota and self-hosted compute are constrained resources even when incremental API billing is zero. Reserve an evaluation budget before generation, so another completed run cannot leave its decisive labels unfunded.

Supervised calibration or adaptation remains a possible later direction. Previous near-chance prompted selectors do not prove that a predictor trained on independent outcome labels cannot work. But the project currently lacks the labels and held-out discrimination evidence that would justify such an investment. Training on another model's confident preferences would reproduce the judgement problem. A small calibrated model or retriever should precede an expensive fine-tuning programme only if the data actually support that simpler test.

**Industrial results support investing in feedback, but do not validate another prompted critic.** RovoDev's actionability component was trained on more than 50,000 historical comments paired with resolution outcomes. Its factual-correctness judge and actionability component had different measured effects. This is evidence for an empirical learning loop, not for copying the number of stages; its results are specific to Atlassian and its resolution metric is not Mathlib request-worthiness. [RovoDev paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.01129v1).

BitsAI-CR likewise uses trained rule-checking and filtering models and revises rules using production feedback. Its line-change metric is an imperfect adoption proxy, particularly in light of this project's own finding that code often changes for unrelated reasons. [BitsAI-CR paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.15134v1). AutoCommenter demonstrates a narrower learned best-practice task deployed with developer feedback, rather than a general solution to selecting worthwhile mathematical API changes. [AutoCommenter paper](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~rjust/publ/code_review_automation_aiware_2024.pdf). The transferable lesson is to connect a clearly defined task to independent outcomes and iterative calibration.

**The outcome contract should make an improvement observable before the next generation run.** I would make the primary comparison the number of distinct useful comments per PR, subject to a predeclared allowance for wrong requests and reviewer burden, at a stated resource budget. Keep historical ask recovery as a separate, valuable measure. Avoid choosing utility weights after seeing which system wins.

For each sampled PR, pool the final delivered comments from both conditions, hide their origin and label them in context. Include baseline-only findings so losses count. Record technical validity, usefulness, scope, and optional versus required status; distinguish uncertainty from rejection. Deduplicate only materially equivalent actions. Then assess whether the complete review introduces repetitive demands, loses useful observations or consumes more reader time. A list of individually plausible comments can still be a worse review.

A bounded first sitting can use existing outputs; no generation is needed. Label complete pooled outputs for a small set of PRs and a separate panel of difficult boundaries. The latter calibrates the rubric, while a random PR sample estimates performance. If only a sample of findings is labelled, retain selection probabilities and report unlabelled coverage. Neither a handpicked interesting-additions panel nor agreement between two similar model readers estimates population precision.

Model judges can assist with matching and triage after calibration. They should not define success solely by agreeing with themselves about taste. Audit semantic matches on partial requests, compound comments, alternative valid fixes and neighbouring locations. Keep evaluator version, reference unit, output channel and denominator attached to every number. The older atomic-obligation judge and the newer raw-ask matcher are not interchangeable measurements.

**The code needs a stable experimental entry point and narrower responsibilities, not another general framework.** The task/data refactor is already integrated into `develop`. It provides sealed session plans, retained matcher inputs, measurement identities, input digests, explicit missingness and durable observations. The `DESIGNS` registry provides an existing extension point for reviewer behaviour. These should remain the foundation.

The comparison/reporting change is more useful than reorganizing directories. `analysis/reports.py` currently centralizes repetition mean, union and stability; it does not provide the proposed paired-PR uncertainty analysis. Keep experiment-specific analysis beside its study, but stop duplicating result loading, matching or aggregation. Stable raw evidence should survive retirement of its runner.

Two output details deserve explicit attention. First, the current solo path projects findings onto work units, and unresolved anchors do not enter final findings. The 26 losses in the latest study are a real delivery limitation, though they do not explain its primary misses. A file-level or PR-level comment channel could preserve legitimate contextual findings, with location matching kept separate; apply any change symmetrically to both conditions. Second, adjudication reuse currently keys on PR, change and issue kind. The code documents collisions between different actions at the same site. Reusing labels should require checked action equivalence or exact exemplars, rather than treating shared location and category as identical judgement.

**The research record also needs reconciliation.** `PROJECT-STATUS.md` still contains strong early claims that the bottleneck is selection and that the model difference is a flagging threshold. Later records qualify those claims. Some todo entries say a rubric is unbuilt although later branches contain frozen panels. `questions.md` predates the latest bank and focused results. A researcher following different entry documents can therefore reach different next steps.

Keep the historical measurements and corrections, but identify one current synthesis and a small active set of decisions. Each experiment should end with its actual reading, incomplete evaluation, and reopening condition. “Failed to pass this advancement rule” should not become “this entire mechanism cannot work”; equally, a new name should not reopen an unchanged failed mechanism. The current collection of worktrees preserves valuable evidence, but result closure should not require rediscovering branch-local files.

**The next programme should have four bounded steps.**

1. **Close measurement and integration gaps using existing artifacts.** Establish the common code revision, reconcile the main conclusions, audit set exposure, and complete a bounded blinded usefulness reading of existing baseline/treatment outputs. If the full 491-item evaluation is not completed, record the reduced scope explicitly. Do not call its interesting-additions subset an overall quality result.
2. **Test one decision-learning premise.** Freeze independent explicit decisions, meaningful contrary cases, the evidence source and the output distinctions. Use the existing fixed-candidate diagnostic only to decide whether the evidence teaches a transferable boundary. End the line if source quality or transfer fails; do not spend a fresh whole-PR campaign repairing familiar examples.
3. **Screen one integrated candidate on broader validation.** Use the strongest established solo route, the same environment and evaluator, preselected PRs including no-ask and AI-generated contributions, and a bounded resource comparator where needed. Prefer breadth, with a smaller random replication subset. Complete the outcome assessment before opening another variant.
4. **Confirm once on reserved material.** Freeze the candidate and advancement margins before the test. Advance only for useful gains that are not concentrated in one PR and do not violate the burden or wrong-request allowance. An inconclusive interval permits only the preregistered extension; a failed result closes the tested intervention. Do not revise it against the test answers.

The minimum worthwhile gain and acceptable burden should be chosen for the intended workflow before running step 3. Estimate the necessary sample size from existing PR-level variation. A proposal such as “one extra useful comment every five PRs” is a decision threshold to discuss, not a result or an established power calculation. If the available budget cannot resolve the desired improvement, narrow the target to an independently defined contribution class or explicitly study equal quality at lower cost. Do not silently change the success criterion after a null result.

I would pause generic specialist expansion, repeated critic/ensemble variants, broader prose banks, full-PR reconstruction, and inference of demand from unasked code. Narrow technical checks remain useful for verified defects and preparation efficiency, but their known coverage does not support another claim that they solve whole-PR review. Persistent Lean checking is a plausible efficiency project only when it frees budget for the selected study; it is not evidence of improved judgement.

The project has substantial reusable results: reviewed-state reconstruction, dated evidence access, documented measurement failures, conditional knowledge effects, a strong solo comparator and concrete examples separating construction from request value. Its next contribution should establish whether a specific use of prior feedback improves delivered review on independent PRs. Achieving that requires fewer simultaneous interventions and a completed assessment of their consequences.

If the decision diagnostic and a properly measured transfer experiment both fail, I would end this version of the harness-improvement programme. The remaining choices are explicit: narrow to a demonstrated useful subtask, study cost reduction, acquire richer outcome feedback, or present a careful empirical account of the limits of the tested methods. Another broad architecture should require new evidence. A reliable negative result is a research contribution, but it should not be relabelled as having achieved improvement over solo review.
