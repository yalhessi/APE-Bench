## 1. The strongest variant

Not dossiers-per-convention, but a two-part design: **(A) precedent retrieval as the selector of which conventions are in play** (nearest census triples — code, ask, merged fix — for each changed hunk, their conventions delivered with the examples under DP's precedence lead), plus **(B) dossiers only for the head's judgement-type conventions, examined by a paired before/after exam**. Why: every positive result needed the oracle's pinpointing (C's exact +0.28, DE +0.23, DP +0.15/+0.18), and every ask-free delivery was ~0 (statute, F +0.03, G +0.000, detectors' volume-only) [read]. Recall now hinges on an ask-free selector, and retrieval is the only one the record leaves open: dead-ends lists "precedent-based selection" as untried and Mode A reopens only for "recognition mode, ranking" [read]. DP's own lead — "a situation like an example's" — is a similarity test; retrieval operationalises it. And DP did nothing on the head: by census rank band DP1 − DE1 is 0 on top-10, +4 on 11-50, −1 on 51-100, +4 on >100 [my count, fact sheet]; all 8 top-10 probe asks are `standard` entries (P005, P011, P018, P063) where DP1 reaches 3, against 11 of 16 above rank 100. So the lever covers the tail once selected; the head needs something else, which is where dossiers belong.

## 2. Variants

**V1. Retrieval as the selector.** *Mechanism:* index the census asks' hunks (1,833, PRs disjoint from the probes); for each changed hunk take top-k; build a ≤ ~5-entry brief a PR, each entry with its triple and DP's lead; the reviewer files where its code is like an example's situation. *For:* short beats long — in stage F, entries delivered among 1-5 sentences yielded 18 of 22 asks, among 25 yielded 12 of 22 [read]; precedent hit@5 49% [read]; DP gained asks no earlier condition reached (30458 `variable`, 32875 `grind`) [read]. *Against:* Mode A priming null (13 vs 11 covered); H's `retrieve` decider AUC 0.59; the 49% was the bench's self-built embeddings, never measured on the shipped index [read]. *Why it may not transfer:* Mode A was older gpt models, no precedence lead, no convention labels; H asked which flagged places maintainers raise (demand), V1 uses retrieval for supply. *Ceiling:* 85% of probe asks are in census (39/46) [read]; at ~49% retrieval hit ≈ 0.4 of asks get a relevant precedent [inferred]; bounded under by the oracle's own DP reach 0.62. *Falsifier (CPU only, $0):* for the 39 asks, does the PR's top-5 retrieved entries contain the ask's entry? ≥ 15/39 proceed; ≤ 8 → retrieval cannot replace the oracle. (Stage F's selector had 6 of 33 within its first 5.)

**V2. Study-and-exam dossiers for head standards.** *Mechanism:* DeepSeek studies a convention's census asks on half the census PRs, writes a dossier (fix forms, how to read a failed check, scope), is examined on the other half with paired cases (asked hunk → flag + fix; its merged after-code → silence), and revises on study-half failures only. *For:* ~4-9 of DP1's 16 misses look like understanding — "check failed" (4: the `gcongr` ask was a *question*; v0092/v0093 tried a failing form); scope (2: p0010 local siblings; p0016 `homMk`/`mkHom` counts); partial or wrong place (3); 30458 shows library-wide scope deciding where file scope misleads [read]. DP's examples never carried the merged fix — census bodies are comment + hunk [read, `materialise_bodies.py`]. *Against:* H's `examples` decider AUC 0.64; G's rule writers already had four asks a rule [read]. *Why it may not transfer:* H and G tested demand or a verify-and-drop lead, not fix forms. *Ceiling:* top-10 = 22% of principled asks, 15% under standard-form entries [counted]; on the probe set at most +5/46 ≈ +0.11 — below one rep's resolution, so only the exam has power here.

Already the static-analysis thread's; the dossier adds only how to read a failure.

**V4. Coarse dossiers (family or mechanism, 12-~30).** *For:* 100% coverage; unbriefed Claude reaches 9 of 16 tail asks and DeepSeek DX 1 of 16 [counted], so DeepSeek's tail gap looks like knowledge a coarse text could carry. *Against:* statute null at N=115; 25 sentences diluted in F [read]. *Falsifier:* family dossiers vs DX on DeepSeek. **V5. The exam as instrument and optimiser, not authority.** Use it to choose a representation per convention and as V2's revision objective. Adds no recall by itself.

## 3. Four questions
(a) Operationally yes: hold the filing lever at maximum, fix the place, force a filing; what remains is departure + fix = understanding. Under DP1 the residual is 9 decision-shaped vs 7 application-shaped (2 of the 9 arguably scope), so in the oracle regime understanding is ~half the residual. In the ask-free regime selection dominates (F/G), and an exam cannot see selection.
(b) Unknown in the record; by construction it can predict only application-given-convention-and-place (the oracle regime). Testable: the exam must reproduce DE < DP given each condition's text, and per-entry exam scores should rank DP's per-ask reach over 3 reps at AUC ≥ 0.70 (~39 asks gives ±0.09, so coarse).
(c) For per-convention dossiers as *the* recall lever, fatal beyond the head (top 50 = 54.6% of principled asks; 286 conventions have ≤ 3 asks). For the combination, no: the tail needs selection (V1), the head needs understanding/execution (V2/V3) — different caps.
(d) Free: apply the rep-1 miss classification to DP's stable misses when reps 2-3 land (already an also-read of the stage). If application-shaped misses ≤ 1/3, understanding is a minor term on this model.

## 4. What I would drop
- Unasked situations as dossier content: H's deciders, told the base rate, set p ≈ 0.05-0.10 everywhere; feeding it in teaches suppression, the opposite of DP's lever.
- Exam-calibrated strict authority as a recall design: validity was never the gap (detector facts 19/20 valid); demand was (maintainers asked at 4-9% of flagged places); under the disposition paragraph gpt-5.5 filed 13 of 14 unasked facts — strict application reproduces that volume outcome.
- The 438-entry index opened like skills: 25 sentences already diluted in F, and "all 438" was not run for that reason.
- The full map (top 50 × 4 representations): it measures the exam, not recall. Keep only head-standards × 3 representations.
- Revealed preference from later Mathlib history, from the first slice: off-goal (it bears on precision), and next to the Δ dead end ("never as ground truth"). Not identical (post-merge, library-wide, not within-PR), but confounded by migrations.
