# R2 — measurement lens (final report as returned, markdown layout normalised; 126k subagent tokens, 27 tool calls, 8.4 min)

**1. Verdict: narrow.** As specified, the exam would mostly measure itself: it hands over the convention and the place, the two things stages F and G show review fails at; its separately scored groups read filing propensity as understanding; and nothing at the available sizes can check a per-convention score against recall. What survives is a threshold-free exam on minimal pairs with a no-convention floor, validated first at the ask level against review reach already on disk. Drop the per-cell map, the authority calibration and the scored third group.

**2. Strongest objections, ranked**

1. **Handing over the place removes the bottleneck the record measured.** Stage F (blind-picked conventions, +0.03) and G (a candidate at the ask's place for 21 asks; G reached 9 = X, README Result 5 application table). The proposed use re-imports selection ("opens a dossier when it judges one relevant", as with skills = stage F). *Refuted if* exam scores predict reach under a non-leaky brief (e.g. a dossier index on census PRs).
2. **Separately scored groups confound understanding with willingness to file.** Asked items are all-positive and conforming all-negative, so any representation that raises filing propensity lifts "asked" and costs "conforming"; DP's bundled precedence paragraph is exactly such a push (+0.15/+0.18). Read separately, the asked gain looks like understanding. Needs pair ordering/AUC with propensity reported separately. *Refuted if* a representation raises asked with conforming held.
3. **Cross-convention validation against recall is infeasible.** Only stage L's 46 probe asks carry census labels outside the census (grep: only `readers/labels_{1,2}.jsonl`). Entries with ≥12 census PRs (31 entries, 43% of val1000 principled asks; my count) hold 14 of 39 in-census probe asks on 7 entries; P018's 5 sit on 2 PRs (4 on 32875). My simulation: a perfect exam gives observed Spearman ≈ 0.55 against reach; power 0.36 at 7 entries, 0.57 at 11 top-50 entries.
4. **The map's cells are unreadable.** PR-split held-out items: the top 10 conventions have 9-24 each, ≤7 below rank ~30; many PR-level asks have no anchored hunk (P043 7/20 anchored, P041 4/16). Paired MDE (80% power, 30% discordant) 0.44 at 12 items, 0.34 at 20, 0.20 at 56 — only P005 (56) and P019 (54) approach that. Add reader agreement 0.809, `unsure` up to 16/54 (P019), and the judge's ~90% self-agreement. Readable outputs are perhaps three: the representation effect pooled over the head (~170 items, ±0.13), rule vs standard, a pooled tail.
5. **No floor arm, and memorisation.** The lowest arm is the sentence, not a hunk-only arm. The census merges (2025-06..2026-03) predate DeepSeek's 2026-09-10 release, so a "merged fix" may be recalled, not derived. Post-2026-04 asks are only outside_years: 248 principled, ≤10 per entry.
6. **The third group is circular.** Mined by a model pinpointer (stage H: 205 flagged places on 16 PRs, 12.8/PR, 6 at an ask = 2.9%), validity unknown; filing there = understanding × strictness, silence = misunderstanding or the maintainers' bar. Uninterpretable.
7. **Likely ceiling.** E reached 17 of 21 asks with a candidate at the place; detector facts valid 19 of 20. The informative range is probably the `standard` half of the codebook (828 of 1,669 asks).

**3. The four questions**
(a) Separable in measurement only if the exam's response is not a filing decision: classification under an explicit not-a-review framing, plus generation of the conforming form, scored threshold-free. Under the proposed groups, no. The record already shows understanding present and filing absent (stage G `{D : ℤ}` delivered as *departs* → "Mathlib does not require these"; DP later reached p0007).
(b) As specified, only itself. The one feasible validation is ask-level: the exam on the 39 probe asks cross-tabulated with DX/DE/DP reach.
(c) Not fatal for the exam as science (31 examinable entries; the tail pooled leave-one-out). Nearly fatal as a recall lever: DP1's understanding-plausible misses at examinable conventions (v0105, p0005, p0003, v0053, v0056; the ambiguous failed checks v0092, v0093) are 5-7 of 46 asks, ≤0.11-0.15, under the leaky exact brief and before stage F's selection discount (one rep; README non-blind classification).
(d) Item 5, on the probe asks themselves.

**4. Reject outright**
- Exam-calibrated authority: the exam never measures selection; maintainers raise ≤5% of flagged places (Result 6); under the disposition paragraph gpt-5.5 filed 13 of 14 unasked facts (dead-ends, detectors). Strict application of a passed convention = volume.
- Scoring the third group as understanding or pass (objection 6).
- Per-cell claims from the map (objection 4).
- Prevalence at base as a decision rule: DP1's p0016 counted `homMk` ~25 files vs `mkHom` ~7 → "NOT a clear naming violation" where the maintainer asked; the linter dead-end shows conventions ramp. Keep prevalence as information to test.
- Revealed preference as a "welcome"/precision score. It differs from Δ (post-merge, community, not author edits under review) but still conflates author taste and adds sweeps (P047's `λ`→`fun` is a sweep target), refactors and drift. It would need a per-convention conformance check, control places (conforming and unflagged situations) for the base change rate, move/rename tracking, and a fixed horizon. The local Mathlib clone has full history (56,957 commits) but ends 2026-05-14, so the val1000 horizon is 2-11 months. It may order the adjudication queue, never score (todo README "Was it acted on"; Δ dead-end "never as ground truth").
- "Proposes the merged fix" scored by the semantic judge alone: fixes are multi-form; compile/equivalence first, `partial` reported.
- Any map without a hunk-only floor column.

**5. First experiment: an ask-level exam on stage L's 39 in-census asks.** Arms: none (hunk only), DE's sentence, DP's section. T1: the pre-review hunk and the merged code at the same place, each shown separately, graded "departs" probability, scored as pair ordering; T2: generate the conforming form. Three reps, not-a-review framing. Pre-stated: over DP1's in-census misses (15/16; or DP1-3's stable misses once reps 2-3 are matched), **drop the dossier-as-recall-lever** (narrow to decision/selection) if ≥ 2/3 of them pass under the DP arm (correct ordering and an accepted fix); **proceed to the head-convention slice** if ≤ 1/3 pass and ≥ 0.8 of reached asks pass; else inconclusive. **Exam uninformative on this model** if the none arm orders ≥ 0.6 of pairs. n≈15 gives ±0.24, so only the extremes read. Cost: ~700 short DeepSeek calls on `first`, under one server-hour after the gate; compile-check the ~11 proof asks, one blind matcher for the rest (~100-150k subscription tokens, can wait for Friday's reset). Unverified: that the PR store holds the merged code at each ask's place (inferred).
