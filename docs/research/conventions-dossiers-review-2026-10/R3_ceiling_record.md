# R3 — ceiling, cost and the record (final report as returned, markdown layout normalised; 147k subagent tokens, 37 tool calls, 9.0 min)

**1. Verdict: narrow — don't build dossiers or exams yet.** The plan as written measures first where the record says the bottleneck is not, and cannot connect that measurement to recall. The tail cap is not fatal the way it was for detectors (examinable conventions hold ~half of census asks vs 2.9%), but the binding step is ask-free *in-context → filed*, and every delivery tried there moved nothing (F +0.03, G +0.00, H AUC 0.56-0.64). A dossier changes the *representation*; it does not supply the *assertion* that carried the gain in every exact-brief condition. One cheap run tests that without building anything (§5).

**2. Objections, ranked**

1. **The realistic recall ceiling is near the detector line's.** From the fact sheet (verified against the README): conventions with ≥8 census asks (53 entries, 56% of census asks) hold 19 of 39 in-census probe asks; the top 50 hold 18 (39% of all 46). DX1 reaches 4 of those 18, Claude X 5.5, DP1 10. Theoretical max (perfect retrieval and application): (18−4)/46 = **+0.30**. Optimistic: F's retrieval (22/33) × DP-level application (6/18) ≈ **+0.09**. On-record ask-free: F kept 1.5 of E's 7.5-ask gain on retrieved asks (20%) → **≈ +0.02**. *Refuted if* any ask-free delivery clears +0.10 (§5).
2. **The first slice (head-10) is where the record shows understanding isn't the problem.** Top-10 band: DE1 = DP1 = 3/8; examples + precedence gained nothing there. DP1's misses there are check-failed (v0092, v0093 P018), merits (v0105 P011, p0005 P063), swept-not-at-ask (v0067 P005): golf search and judgement — the "try-it" mechanism (19 of 67 residual, `reviewer-model-gap.md:533`). All of DP's top-50 gain was in ranks 11-50 (3→7). *Refuted if* a sentence-only exam on P005/P018/P011 scores low and a dossier raises it.
3. **The gains measured so far are in the tail, which cannot be examined.** Ranks >100: DX1 / DE1 / DP1 = 1 / 7 / 11 of 16. Top 50: 4 / 6 / 10 of 18. Exams need held-out cases; the tail has ≤3 census asks per entry.
4. **The exam scores the non-bottleneck and excludes the bottleneck.** Stage H's bottleneck was selection among departures; the exam treats unasked departures as volume, not error. Departure-vs-conformance is already mostly solved (detector facts 19/20 valid). The exam's asked/conforming discrimination is stage H's `examples` design under another score.
5. **Leakage biases toward a false null.** Census merged fixes are in Mathlib master, likely in DeepSeek's training data. If sentence-only already produces the fix, both conditions saturate. Needs a pre-registered saturation stop (sentence-only ≥ 0.8 on the asked group → the slice can't discriminate).
6. **Per-convention exams have no power.** Only 18 entries have ≥ 20 asks (32% of census asks); rank 50 has 8 asks → ~4 held-out cases, one case = 25pp. Reader A's `unsure` is 20.6%, 107 entries broadened, so many entries mix mechanisms.
7. **"Passed → apply strictly" is the disposition paragraph again.** Stage 5's pre-registered gate rejected it as a volume lever (5/13 asks, 13 of 14 unasked facts filed, `dead-ends.md:297`); stage H puts maintainers' raise rate ≤ 5% of flagged places. The user must rule on whether volume is acceptable under the recall-only goal before this step means anything.
