# S — steelman (final report as returned, markdown layout normalised; 145k subagent tokens, 32 tool calls, 6.8 min)

**1. The strongest design.** Situation-scoped exhaustive checks (SSC): split the PR into typed anchors (each check still sees the whole PR text and diff, so the part narrows *where to look*, not what the model can read); each census entry declares the part types it applies to and a conformance-blind trigger; every entry whose trigger holds fires (not a top-k); each (part, entry) pair gets one free DeepSeek turn in the ladder's U form ("does this place conform; if not, the change"); every `departs` is filed and unioned with the unbriefed solo reviewer. It is the strongest because it is the only design consistent with all three ask-free results: stages F and G failed at the agentic session's decision, not at retrieval (G: the right entry at the right place for 21 of 33 asks, reach 9 = X) (read); the ladder's bare sentence at the place writes 6 of DP's 7 gains (read, provisional, unmatched); and SSC removes the session's filing bar by construction while answering the detectors' stopping reason (2.9% coverage) with census-wide coverage.

**2. The designs.**

**D1 — SSC.** *Mechanism:* leaf parts from the change graph's named targets (declaration, doc_comment, attribute, import, module_doc, section/variable), plus family, file and PR containers; each entry gets a `visits` list of part types, a trigger, `not_when` and `check_kind` (stage G's rule-writer rubric as the template); syntactic triggers run in code, semantic ones as one batched yes/no call per part; then the U-form check per pair; then file every `departs`, union, rank. *For:* of the 52 `rules.json` triggers, about 25 can be decided from syntax (my reading, inferred); the census's rule/standard split is about half (841/828 asks); rules fire on a median of about 3 of 10 PRs (computed from `pinpoints/`); U says `departs` at 93 of 117 asked places. *Against:* stage H (maintainers raise 1 in 20 to 1 in 400 of flagged places); the detectors under disposition (13 of 14 unasked facts filed); no unasked place tested in the ladder; G's rules pooled knowing the asks. *Ceiling,* stacking measured factors: in census 0.85 (probe) or 0.78 (211 of 269 held-out other-years asks; computed) × at the place 0.64 (G, pooled, so an upper bound) × departs 0.79 ≈ at most 0.40-0.43 of all asks for the engine alone, against DX's ~0.19. *False filing:* ~70-100 pairs a PR (inferred from G's 385 candidates for 52 hot rules on 10 PRs), so plausibly 10-30 filings per ask. *Containment:* run the compile or lookup a check needs before filing; turn a failed fix into a question, not a drop (G lost `gcongr` by dropping); cap per part, ordered by the pass's own departure order (stage H's best: 7 of 12 at three a PR). *Falsifier:* §5, then U at unasked places.

**D2 — per-part shrink to 5-10, delivered DP-style to sub-reviews.** *Mechanism:* part-type filter → MiniLM nearest census hunks or a cheap DeepSeek ranker → 5-10 entries with examples and precedence per part sub-review. *For:* DP moved DeepSeek +0.20 over DE (read); per-part rather than per-PR selection is untested. *Against:* stage F's whole-PR Opus selector: top 5 held the entry for 6 of 33 asks, top 10 for 15, top 25 for 22, entries at ranks 7-22; 25 delivered sentences gave +0.03 with asks reached a finding falling to 0.18; precedent priming delivered but did not transfer. *Ceiling:* 0.85 × hit@10 × DP application (~0.64): at F's 0.45 ≈ 0.25; at hit@10 0.7 ≈ 0.38. *False filing:* modest (DP +0.62 findings a PR over DE). *Falsifier:* per-part hit@10 ≥ 0.60 on held-out asks.

**D3 — sub-reviews by family, or by (reference, object) cell.** *Mechanism:* one agentic sub-review per family or §7.5 cell, holding that group's entries. *For:* an entry's asks are 90% inside its majority family (1,507 of 1,669; computed), so family is a good index field; the §7.5 cell predicts how an ask is settled on 85% of asks. *Against:* the family division (1 of 10 obligations against the ten arms' 3); specialists (104 of 136 abstentions `already_correct`); F1 has 89 entries and F6 92, too many for 5-10; rationale does not shrink (each of the top five rationales tags 25-29% of asks; correctness tags 34). *Ceiling:* about X's (inferred from F's dilution). *Falsifier:* an F8 naming sub-review (36 entries) on DeepSeek against DX on naming asks, three reps.

**D4 — D1 with D2's ranker used only to order filings:** volume containment for D1, not a filter; used if D1 passes.

**3. Q1 and Q2, from the idea's strongest position.**

**Q1.** Decompose into anchors, not contexts. Every changed line lies in exactly one leaf part, and every leaf in a file and the PR container, so coverage of where work can be anchored holds by construction; PR-grain asks (14%) and unit-spanning ones (3 of 42) have their containers; the 12 of 15 multi-declaration asks an arm saw but could not say together need a family part that files once for the group; "judgements that need the whole file" survive because the context is never cropped. Grain coverage is measurable on CPU: the share of held-out asks whose part type is in their entry's `visits`.

**Q2.** Probably yes for *is this convention in play*; no for *would a maintainer raise it* (stage H AUC 0.56-0.64). So the strongest version chooses only the first: it selects on "does the situation occur here", by syntax where possible and a semantic call otherwise, and does not predict demand.

**The census, made robust for D1.** Each entry gets `visits` from a closed set of part types; a trigger tagged syntactic or semantic; `not_when`; `check_kind`; scope by area and era; maturity (D2's norm store). Validate triggers out of sample: each must fire at its own held-out asks' places (the 211 other-years asks), with firing density measured on census PRs as stage H did. Merge the readers' duplicates via the cross-map (312 `same` pairs). Finish the paused saturation reading (7.1% new so far, projected ~115). Give the 286 entries with ≤ 3 asks a semantic trigger tied to their part type, not a syntactic one fitted to one to three examples.

**Where the steelman fails.** *Demand:* a perfect situation index and a perfect departure judge still buy recall through volume; every stage's pre-registered guards (findings ≤ X + 3; asks reached per finding ≥ X's) almost surely fail, as the detectors' did, so D1 inherits the detectors' reopen condition — an adjudication of whether unasked filings are welcome — and until then its gain cannot be told from volume. *No measured discrimination:* if U says `departs` at unasked places at ~79%, the check only passes the trigger through. *Tools:* 19 of the 26 pool rules need a compile or a lookup, and the ladder's f-class misses (v0105, v0092/v0093, v0067) need tools. *The out-of-census remainder:* 15-22% of asks have no entry, and one new entry still arrives per ten asks. *Every ceiling factor comes from 33-39 asks.*

**4. Drop from the draft.**
- Rationale as a scoping key: it does not shrink (top five rationales each tag 25-29% of asks); "correctness" is 34 asks.
- Agentic sub-reviews per part: F, G, the family division, the specialists' silence.
- DP's examples in the engine: UX 88 `departs` against U's 93, three s asks flipped to `conforms`, and the sentence alone carried 6 of 7 gains.
- A hard top-5-10 cut: at F's ranks the entries behind asks sit at 7-22.
- "Scrutinise afterwards" meaning verify-and-drop: G lost 8 asks with a candidate at the place that way; scrutiny for validity only, a failed check turned into a question.
- Any attempt to predict demand (stage H).

**5. First measurement: per-part situation recall on held-out asks** (CPU only, minutes, $0, no tokens). *Sets:* the 211 other-years asks reader A assigned to val1000 entries (held out of the codebook's construction; `materialise_bodies.py` gives each ask's path, line and hunk; the PR store gives the diff), and the 39 probe asks as a second set. *Steps:* tag each ask's commented line with a part type (a parser over the diff); keep the entries whose majority family maps to that part type (expected retention ~0.90); rank survivors by nearest val1000 hunks of the same part type (the planned V1 retrieval, per part rather than per PR); report hit@5/10/25 and survivors per part. *Thresholds, fixed now:* **hit@10 ≥ 0.60** on the 211 (F's whole-PR Opus selector: 0.45) → the per-part shrink is real; write triggers for the full index (D2/D4). **hit@25 < 0.67** (no better than F's 25) → syntax plus retrieval does not shrink soundly; only D1 without a top-k remains, its cost the pair count from the same run. Between → mixed; read where the misses sit by part type. *Second, also free:* U on DeepSeek at stage H's 199 unasked flagged places against its asked ones (~2 h on `first`); `departs` at unasked places ≥ 0.7 → D1 is a detector with a model in it.
