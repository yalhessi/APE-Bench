# R1 — Q1, decomposition and coverage (final report as returned, markdown layout normalised; 145k subagent tokens, 51 tool calls, 9.7 min)

**1. Verdict.** The record supports overlapping lenses, not a partition: four lenses — the declaration (bundled with its doc-comment and attributes), the family, the file and the PR — with library search as a tool in every lens, not a unit. `components.py:1-24, 266-390` already builds these (site, introduction, family, file, intent), and a target may sit in several. Splitting a part further by what changed in it (proof, name, statement, docs, attributes, layout; `ModificationComponent`, `identity.py:400-420`) is what shrinks the conventions. A partition covers every ask by construction, which tells us nothing (`review-coordination-design-2026-09.md` §6.5). The real limits are three: whether the part has the context the ask needs, whether its shrunk list holds the ask's principle, and whether one decision spread across parts can be said as one. Every figure below is read off disk; "computed" marks my own exploratory counts, not pre-registered.

**2. Objections to step 1, ranked**

1. **No evidence that splitting the review adds recall on any reviewer tried; the measured failure is the decision to file, not attention.** The coordination passes (a decomposition by object), run by hand on Claude, reached 5-6 of 11 asks, as did the plain brief (`review-coordination-design-2026-09.md:441-452`); "the pipeline's own structure adds nothing over gpt_5.2 solo on unseen PRs" (`todo/README.md:108`); stage G had the right convention at the right place for 21 asks and the model checked and overruled it. Decomposition fixes attention; the record points at disposition. The shrink can also be delivered per place to a single whole-PR reviewer, as stages F and G did. *Refuted by* a matched probe where the same per-part conventions reach more asks as concurrent part reviews than as one whole-PR packet.
2. **About 45% of asks need context from outside one part.** Computed on the 600 random facet asks, my priority bucketing (readers agree within a point): local to one part of one declaration 55-56%; family or siblings 17-18%; library 12%; design of a definition, instance or meta code 6%; PR 5-6%; file or location 4%. On the census asks' grain labels (computed): line or declaration 71% (both readers) to 77% (either); declaration group 8-12%; file 4-7%; PR 9-12%. The record has the same: 24% need more than one declaration (`review-coordination-design:231`).
3. **AI-generated PRs, the target population, lean further outside parts** (computed): PR grain 17.8-19.0% on AI PRs against 9.4% on the rest; comments not on a line 20.5% against 10.8%; only 49-53% of AI-PR asks are local to one part.
4. **Asks about several declarations fail at emission even when the unit is right** (gpt-5.2 and Qwen only). In 12 of 15 multi-declaration asks the arm saw every target and could not say them together (`todo/batching-work-units.md`); once the `patch_set` channel was fixed and callable the reviewer filed none in either run, against a baseline of 0 of 3,411 (`2a1f1c95`: "the channel was never the constraint"); the judge matches one candidate per obligation, so a decision split across parts scores as unresolved. Finer parts make this worse. Untested on Opus or DeepSeek.
5. **Each part multiplies the places where unasked filings can land.** About 12-13 asks per 100 changed declarations (structure doc §1.2); maintainers raise a convention at 1 in 20 to 1 in 400 of its places (stage H); forcing raised control-PR emission from 1 to 36 findings. The decomposition sets the denominator step 4 must then scrutinise.

**3. Proposal.** The four lenses above; issue comments and review bodies anchored to the PR lens; the reviewer sees the PR diff (no arm does today, which the record lists as a defect); each part's shrunk list keyed by (lens, changed parts, declaration kind).

*Expected convention coverage* (computed; frequency-ranked census entries, train and test halves split by PR):

| key | top 5 | top 10 | top 25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| none (global) | 12% | 20% | 35% |
| object the ask changes (part-level proxy) | 38% | 46% | 52% |
| ask's own family (an oracle, upper bound) | 38% | 50% | 62% |

So a syntactic key buys about 2.5× over none at 10 entries and reaches about half the asks alone (stage F's blind selector: 15 of 33 in its first 10, 45%). *Conventions mostly belong to one object:* principles with ≥ 3 sample asks sit 84% on their modal object (computed); the object predicts the family for proof (94% F1), prose (94% F10), layout (92% F9), binders (91% F2), name (85% F8) and PR (100% F12), but not for statements (F6 50%, F7 29%) or families (F6 68%) — where the design conventions spread (F6 has 92 entries, its top 5 cover 34%). *Ceiling:* 8.9% of asks have no principle (facts about the PR); about one new ask in ten needs a principle the census lacks (Good-Turing 0.099-0.107); so even perfect selection reaches roughly 82% (inferred). *Leaves out:* those ~18%; asks about code outside the diff ("apply elsewhere", retirement); one decision spread across parts; demand. *Shrink key versus review split:* grouping conventions (family, rationale) and dividing the code differ; the shrink needs only the code-part key to *filter* conventions, not the *review* split by convention group.

**4. Reject outright**
- Splitting by rationale: the five main rationales each carry 416-488 asks (weighted, reader A), so one shrinks nothing; "correctness" is 34 asks (~2% of 1,669); a rationale reviewer is the family division again (1 of 10 obligations against 3, `dead-ends.md:271`, provisional).
- A shrink keyed on declaration shape alone: the facet-keyed join dead end (fit 4-8%).
- Disjoint parts packed by hash and size: families split across units, 47% of units held no declaration (`batching-work-units.md`).
- More concurrent slots as a coverage floor: more slots bought more abstentions (`dead-ends.md:58`).
- Sites taken from gold: the oracle-site dead end.
- Reading `coverage_gaps` as coverage of the work: it counts mandatory jobs that did not succeed (`runner.py:880-960`), whether the plan ran, not whether it was adequate.
