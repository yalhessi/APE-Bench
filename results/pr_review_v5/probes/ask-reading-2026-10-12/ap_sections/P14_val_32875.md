
## Conventions the maintainers hold

These are conventions Mathlib's maintainers hold when they review, one sentence each: a situation and the form preferred in it. They are stated in general, and none names a place in this PR. Check the changed code against each one. Where it departs from a convention, request the change that convention calls for, as a finding at the place it applies.

- A goal that a standard finishing tactic (`simp`, `rfl`, `trivial`, `omega`, `positivity`, `grind`, `grw`, ...) closes is closed by that tactic, not by a hand-written term or rewrite chain.

Take the conventions and these examples as the standard for this review: they show what maintainers ask for, and they outrank your own judgement of this code. Where the changed code is in a situation like an example's, file the change its convention calls for, as a finding at that place. Where you cannot make or check the change, file it as a question to the author that names the convention and the change. Leave a place unfiled only where a line of the code you quote, or a check you ran, shows it already has the form the convention calls for.
