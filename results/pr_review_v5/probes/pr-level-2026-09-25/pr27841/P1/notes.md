1. **Wanted / worth having.** At base, pnat sums appear only in Cotangent.lean (mlgrep "∑' n : ℕ+": 2 hits), and none of the new lemmas has a caller. `tsum_int_eq_tsum_neg` is `(Equiv.neg ℤ).tsum_eq`, which JacobiTheta/TwoVariable.lean:402,445 already use directly. `tprod_zero_pnat_eq_tprod_nat` is a one-line composition of `Multipliable.tprod_eq_zero_mul` (NatInt.lean:244). No exact duplicate exists; the searches for `tsum_comp_neg`, `tprod_comp_inv` and an even-function tsum lemma found nothing.
2. **Every hunk an improvement.** The Cotangent.lean hunk is forced by the signature change, so it is related, not stylistic. The switch of `f` from explicit to implicit breaks callers without being needed for the `R` generalization, and `Equiv.tprod_eq` takes `f` explicitly. Filed as advisory.
3. **One thing.** The title says "add more pnat tsum lemmas", but the PR also generalizes and changes the signatures of two existing lemmas, and adds a non-pnat ℤ lemma. Asked for a retitle or a split.
4. **Description.** It is empty: no motivation, no design rationale for `dont_translate := R` or the implicit `f`, no link to downstream work or discussion, no AI-use disclosure. Blocking.
5. **Finishes the job.** The even-function identity is hand-proved at the HasSum level in JacobiTheta/OneVariable.lean:67-83 and is not refactored. The new lemmas are additive-only, while the file's ℤ-splitting API comes as HasProd, Multipliable and tprod lemmas via `@[to_additive]`. The only other `Equiv.pnatEquivNat` sites are the two lemmas being changed, so no other pnat place was missed. The PR adds no automation or meta code, so there is nothing to test.
6. **Right place.** The pnat lemmas belong in NatInt.lean. `tsum_int_eq_tsum_neg` has nothing specific to ℤ; if kept, it belongs next to `Equiv.tprod_eq` in InfiniteSum/Basic.lean. This is folded into the Q1 finding.
