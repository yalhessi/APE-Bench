# Goal brief

## 1. Kind of contribution

- New results.
- API reorganization: one existing lemma is moved and renamed.

## 2. Capability

Let G be a group and let H, K, N be subgroups of G, with N normal in G. Write [A, B] for the
commutator subgroup of two subgroups (Mathlib's existing `⁅A, B⁆`, generated by the commutators of
elements of A with elements of B), and A ⊔ B for the subgroup generated by A and B. The contribution
provides the distributive law

  [H ⊔ K, N] = [H, N] ⊔ [K, N].

It also reorganizes the existing lemma `Subgroup.normalizer_inf_normalizer_le_normalizer_sup` and its
additive counterpart `AddSubgroup.normalizer_inf_normalizer_le_normalizer_sup`, now in
`Mathlib/Algebra/Group/Subgroup/Pointwise.lean`. For subgroups H, K it says that
N_G(H) ∩ N_G(K) ≤ N_G(H ⊔ K). The lemma is to move to a file earlier in the import order, and to get
a name consistent with the naming of related lemmas already in the library. You choose where it goes
among the files you have and what it is called.

## 3. What must keep working

- The content of the moved lemma, in its multiplicative and its additive form.
- The existing results that use it, `Subgroup.conj_mem_sup_of_mem_inf_normalizer_of_mem_inf` and
  `Subgroup.normalizer_le_normalizer_sup_of_normalizer_le_left` in `Pointwise.lean`, with their
  additive counterparts.
- Code that refers to the lemma by its current names, in Mathlib and downstream, must still resolve.

## 4. Open questions

- Whether the companion law, with the join in the second argument of the commutator, belongs in this
  contribution.
- Whether an additive counterpart of the new law is wanted.
- Whether versions for the join of an indexed family of subgroups are wanted.
- Whether the law should also be available under hypotheses weaker than normality of N in G. The
  inputs state it only for N normal.

## 5. Where the requirements come from

- The distributive law and the hypothesis that N is normal in G: the title (the current title, not
  verified to be the one at review time) and the description's first paragraph. The description is
  verified to be the text at the start of review.
- The move of the normalizer lemma to an earlier file, and its rename for consistent naming: the
  description's third paragraph. The change itself removes the lemma from `Pointwise.lean`.
- The two dependent results that must keep working: the change itself (the PR modifies the proofs of
  `conj_mem_sup_of_mem_inf_normalizer_of_mem_inf` and
  `normalizer_le_normalizer_sup_of_normalizer_le_left`).
- The current names still resolving: the change itself (the PR keeps both old names usable).
- The description's second paragraph is about how the result is proved, and is withheld on purpose.
