# Unary order bounds in the history of InfiniteSum/Order

At the base of PR 42184, both weak bounds on an infinite product by the multiplicative identity have a named witness-level lemma. The strict lower bound uses a binary comparison. The same split appears in the additive code in December 2023. This is one local API lineage. The case and candidate companion were already known when we chose the family. [Reviewed input, PR 42184](../../../../inputs/probes/probe-2026-07-08-v1/input/episodes.jsonl) · [Earlier case investigation](../../../../docs/questions.md)

We fixed one file and four statements up to additive translation. For each side of the identity and each of `≤` or `<`, look for a unary bound on the chosen total (`tsum` or `tprod`) from pointwise bounds on the terms, then look for the corresponding bound on a supplied limit. The strict lower theorem additionally assumes convergence and one strictly larger term. Reading the source statements and proof calls gives the following results at the July 27, 2026 base. [Weak bounds](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/5b3f719bf129e3cf48daf6ff76a2ec5cb6e9b1e3/Mathlib/Topology/Algebra/InfiniteSum/Order.lean#L159-L182) · [Strict comparison](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/5b3f719bf129e3cf48daf6ff76a2ec5cb6e9b1e3/Mathlib/Topology/Algebra/InfiniteSum/Order.lean#L197-L233)

| Subject | Public statement about the total | Implementation in the file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lower weak, all terms ≥ 1 | `one_le_tprod` | Calls `hg.hasProd.one_le h` in the convergent, nonbottom branch. |
| Upper weak, all terms ≤ 1 | `tprod_le_one` | Calls `hf.hasProd.le_one h` in that branch. |

| Upper strict, one term < 1 | No corresponding declaration in this file | No value-level declaration to pair. |

The December 2023 handwritten declarations were `HasSum.nonneg`/`tsum_nonneg`, `HasSum.nonpos`/`tsum_nonpos` and `tsum_pos`. In March 2024 the infinite-products change replaced these definitions with multiplicative source and generated their additive counterparts via `to_additive`. The strict value lemma later moved under `Multipliable`, and general summation filters added bottom-filter branches to the weak proofs. The sampled dates show continuity of where the unary companion is exposed despite those changes. They supply three comparable value-level subjects, with two companions, at each date. They cannot estimate adoption across independent families, reviewer enforcement, or changes between snapshots. For 42184 the submitted theorem bounds a product by the **absorbing zero** under `CommMonoidWithZero` and `PosMulMono`. The older product bounds compare to the **identity one** in an ordered monoid. The relevant analogy is the convergent proof branch, where the caller already has a witness. Whether the extra lemma merits a review comment still requires a judgment about that caller. The strict case shows how another public comparison can already serve a plausible witness-level client. [Submitted patch](../../../../inputs/probes/probe-2026-07-08-v1/input/episodes.jsonl) · [Existing finite-product-to-limit bound](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/5b3f719bf129e3cf48daf6ff76a2ec5cb6e9b1e3/Mathlib/Topology/Algebra/InfiniteSum/Order.lean#L26-L38)

The strict witness client below compiled on the unmodified July 27 base (`lake env lean --stdin`, exit 0). The existing comparison supplies its proof in one call.

```lean
import Mathlib.Topology.Algebra.InfiniteSum.Order
variable {ι α : Type*} {L : SummationFilter ι} [CommGroup α] [PartialOrder α]
  [IsOrderedMonoid α] [TopologicalSpace α] [IsTopologicalGroup α] [OrderClosedTopology α]
  [L.LeAtTop] [L.NeBot] {g : ι → α} {a : α}
example (h : HasProd g a L) (hg : ∀ i, 1 ≤ g i) (i : ι) (hi : 1 < g i) : 1 < a :=
  hasProd_lt (f := fun _ : ι ↦ 1) hg hi hasProd_one h
```

## How often would a submitted diff raise the question?

The narrow screen fired on **one of 390** pre-2026 development PRs and **one of ten** July inputs. It selects a theorem or lemma with a complete visible signature, an added or replaced signature line in a Lean diff hunk and an outer weak inequality. One side must be a numeral and the other must start with `∑'`, `∏'`, `tsum` or `tprod`. The opportunity is to inspect the declaration. The selector uses neither comments from reviewers nor their dispositions. This retrospective feasibility pass followed selection of 42184 and the protocol cases.
