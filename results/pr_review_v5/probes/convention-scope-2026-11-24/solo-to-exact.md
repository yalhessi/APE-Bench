# Where a review design sits between solo and exact convention delivery

On the ten October PRs actually reviewed in the Opus comparison, exact convention selection recovered **11 additional historical requests** over ordinary solo. Selecting 25 sentences from the diff recovered **one additional request on net**, or 9% of that gain. This is the most direct measured position we have for an automatic Opus design.

## Define the endpoints

Use the same PRs, reviewer, tools, submission channel, scoring rule and reading budget. Let **S** be historical requests recovered by ordinary solo, **D** the score with the candidate design, and **O** the score with oracle convention delivery. Report the three scores and their differences. When the measured oracle gain is appreciably positive, the **share of the oracle gain** is `(D − S) / (O − S)`. An extra request gained and a different solo request lost cancel in the numerator. Show both when diagnosing a design. Below-zero and above-one results are possible. When the denominator is small, zero or negative, use the paired differences on their own.

In October the oracle was retrospective. Two readers saw the historical maintainer requests and selected the corresponding entries in a frozen catalogue. The reviewer received the PR-specific list of general situation/preference sentences in catalogue order. It still had to locate the issue, decide what to ask and file it. The selection gave it a strong signal about which practices the human reviewer had actually acted on. This is an empirical reference for recovering recorded requests. A separate question is whether a reviewer can recognize every applicable practice, including one the PR already follows. That requires independently labelled opportunities. The first design recovered two of the ten requests the exact reviewer found and both ordinary runs missed. It also displaced some ordinary hits, which is why its **net** gain was only one. It selected the entry behind 22 of the 33 requests. The exact reviewer recovered 18 of those 22 and the automatic reviewer recovered 12. Selection coverage alone overstates the gain the author gets. The second design started with a favorable pool constructed from the 26 rules implicated by the target requests plus 26 decoys. Only the choice of which rules to apply where was blind. Its exact reference used sentences, so this row measures the complete intervention against the existing oracle. It cannot isolate the effect of selecting locations. **DeepSeek gives a second, approximate reference.** Across those 33 requests and three repeats, its ordinary reviewer reached 16/99 request-by-repeat opportunities. With 25 diff-selected conventions, examples, and an instruction to give them precedence it reached 34/99. With the same delivery format and exact convention selection the earlier matchers credited 61/99 and 60/99. Using each comparison's own solo baseline gives **40% or 39%** of the oracle gain. The exact and automatic arms were judged in different batches. The automatic design also raised comments per PR from 2.70 to 4.87 and missed its registered yield threshold (0.309 to 0.295 historical requests reached per finding). Of the 460 example appearances in those briefs, 173 were at or after the target's cutoff, affecting seven of the ten PRs. Its value using only examples available at review time remains unmeasured. **Reading depth changes the window.** Crediting only original matcher pointers within the first five comments per PR, the same tables give **22%** for the Opus diff shortlist, **13%** for the targeted checks and **24–25%** for DeepSeek. These are retrospective counts of the pointers in full-list judgments; truncated reviews could earn different matches if evaluated directly. In a new comparison, set the reading budget in advance and judge exactly those comments. Report actual comments as well since some reviewers stop early. ## Position of the November design

**Its share of the window is unknown.** On the missing companion in PR 42184, the full-tool pair went from 0/1 to 1/1 in the review channel. The repeated file-only comparison went from 0/2 to 1/2. On that single full-tool review the guided reviewer recovered the only recorded request solo had missed, so its two findings exhaust the two historical requests. That is an observed single-case maximum. The investigator selected the PR and composed the caller question knowing the historical case. Neither route ran a matched exact-delivery arm. October used different prompts and scoring conditions. The guided packet success rate of 1/2 estimates a different quantity from the fraction of the oracle gain recovered. The six-note bank and the later Opus examples-and-precedence comparison also have solo comparisons but no jointly judged, same-format oracle. [November outputs and controls](README.md) · [Earlier studies](prior-evidence.md)

For this design, the three arms on a fresh panel should work as follows:

| Arm | What reaches the whole-PR reviewer |
|---|---|
| Solo | The ordinary PR, repository and tools. |
| Design | From the incoming PR and a date-frozen bank, the selector chooses one convention or abstains. Render the chosen convention as a caller question. On abstention use ordinary solo. |
| Exact delivery | Select the best reference-aligned convention from the **same bank** using offline labels, capped at one. Give the reviewer the **same caller question** the design would render for that convention. The evaluator alone sees the historical requests. |

For a standing benchmark, keep the same solo and exact-delivery reference for every design on that panel. If a design changes the delivery format, retain the common reference and add a version of the design with oracle selection. For example, supply the same oracle-selected convention once as a sentence and once as a caller question. The score with an oracle-selected question measures how well the reviewer can use that format; the drop when the automatic selector chooses the question measures the cost of selection. Match the allowance for the number of selected conventions when making that comparison.

Score the complete policy across arrivals, including abstentions. Report in-catalogue recovery, all expert requests recovered and lost, unsolicited or redundant comments, and actual review cost. Quiet PRs contribute to burden even when they have no historical request. Preserve AI-assisted PRs and source dates. Publish a paired PR-level uncertainty interval for the absolute gains and show review and verified channels separately. On 42184 alone, the historical requests in those channels moved **1 to 2** and **1 to 0**, respectively. [Matched channel replay](full-tool.md)
